**Every Adult Swim show, without the episodes that need a sign-in.**

Adult Swim splits its catalogue between episodes anyone can watch and episodes
that need a TV provider sign-in. This channel holds the same shows as **Adult
Swim Subscription** with the **Adult Swim Subscription** listing filtered out,
so nothing in it asks you to sign in.

Built from the show list on `adultswim.com/videos`, which is checked once a
month for anything new.
